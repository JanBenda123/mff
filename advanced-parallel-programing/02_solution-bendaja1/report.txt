My comments and possible signs of not optimal implementation
 - naive      - I know that naive solution is supposed to be bad. But I did not expect it to 
                be almost 10 times as bad. Is it correct?

 - atomic_shm - It surprised me when no privatization was as good as mild privatization 
                (still not all 32 banks per bin used). That is because of the reduction step (last for loop)
                which may not be optimal, but I did not find any better way of doing this. Can I do better?

 - overlap    - Overlapping did not bring any benefit to it. The bigger the chunck size, the faster it run. 
                In my opinion it's cause the copying takes ~50 times as long as the kernel computation. 

 - final      - I've experimented with cudaMemAdvise a bit. The version I chose is primarily the fastest
                and secondarly which made the most sense. (Some flags did not make a difference but they 
                felt sensible to include).
